VEGA is a course project that reads a few photos and suggests fashion aesthetics. This page checks its suggestions with human eyes.

Each screen shows four photos one person picked and one style suggested for them, with three example pictures of that style. Click Fits, Doesn't fit or Can't tell. That is all.

Say it fits if the example pictures look like the four photos together, or clearly like the one outlined photo. Go with your first impression; no fashion knowledge is needed. Some of the suggestions are deliberately bad, so "doesn't fit" is often the right answer.
